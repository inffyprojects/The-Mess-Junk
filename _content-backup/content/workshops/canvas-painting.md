The session most people book first. An artist walks the room while you work, so
you get a hand when you want one and space when you don't — nobody is going to
stand over your shoulder correcting your sky.

Acrylics, brushes, canvas and an apron are all provided. Most people arrive
certain they can't paint and leave arguing about where to hang it.
