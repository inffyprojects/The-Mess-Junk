Resin is equal parts chemistry and nerve: you mix, you tint, you pour, and then
you stop touching it. Getting that timing right is the whole craft, and it is
much easier to learn in a room with someone who has done it a few hundred times.

Gloves, resin, pigments and moulds are included. The studio is ventilated and we
keep these groups smaller for safety.
